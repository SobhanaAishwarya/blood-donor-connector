Ring-based fan-out

The search expands intelligently — never all at once

When an urgent request is created, the 5 nearest eligible and available donors are contacted first. If no one accepts within 15 minutes, the radius grows to the next ring, then the next.

  • ◦ Ring 1 — 0–5 km: 5 nearest eligible donors
  • ◦ Ring 2 — 5–10 km: next nearest, after 15 min of silence
  • ◦ Ring 3 — 10–20 km: wider area, only if still unmatched
